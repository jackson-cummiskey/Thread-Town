# Thread Town interiors

The creative brief for the twelve department rooms in version 5. docs/CONTRACT_V5.md (section "Interiors") wins over anything here; docs/ART_DIRECTION.md holds the shared palette and material values; docs/CITY_DESIGN.md gives each building the character its room continues. All distances are world units (1 tile = 1 unit), angles in radians, time in seconds, colors sRGB hex. Sentence case, no em dashes anywhere, in code comments or UI copy.

Conventions used below:

- Room `i` (department order in `TT.DEPARTMENTS`: exec, embedded, compliance, fcc, ops, data, credit, finance, tech, risk, marketing, people) has `floorY = -60`, center `cx = 200 + i * 24`, `cz = 0`. World +z is south, as everywhere in the town.
- Positions inside a room are written in **room-local** coordinates `(u, y', v)`: `u` runs east 0..12 from the west wall's inner face, `v` runs south 0..9 from the north wall's inner face, `y'` is height above the floor. World `x = origin.x + u`, world `z = origin.z + v`, world `y = floorY + y'`, with `origin = { x: cx - 6, z: -4.5 }`.
- `// CONTRACT:` the v5 text calls `origin` the south-west floor corner and also defines the walkable rectangle as `origin` to `origin + (w, d)`. Those two statements only agree if `origin` is the minimum-coordinate corner, which in world terms is the north-west corner (`z = -4.5`). `interiors.js` uses the minimum corner for `origin`, so `origin + (12, 9)` is the south-east corner and the south wall (door side) is at `v = 9`. Leave this comment in the module.
- Every helper from `js/depts/common.js` applies: `box`, `cyl`, `cone` and `column` are base anchored (y is the bottom), `sphere`, `torus`, `plane` and `decal` are center anchored. Dimensions are written `w x h x d` (east, up, south). "glow(c, i)" is `common.glow`, "glass()" is one `common.glass()` per room, transparent false, emissive driven to 0.9 for the windows. Shared colors are the ART_DIRECTION values (trim #F8F6F0, iron #2E3E50, wood #A5774E, dark wood #6B4A2B, desk top #E8DCC0, brass #F6D463, bulb #FFF1C9 emissive #FFE2A0, lamp light #FFC97A, paper #E8E2D5, ink #0E1B2E, cork #D8B98A, water #6CC3CB, sand #E3D5B2, hedge #6FA860, stone #B9C4CC, light stone #CBD3DA, plinth #CFC6B6). Each room may add at most two extra colors, listed under its heading.
- Obstacles are axis-aligned rectangles on the floor in room-local `u, v`, converted to world AABBs `{x0, z0, x1, z1}` by adding `origin`. The player radius is 0.28, so keep every walkway at least 1.0 wide. Three lanes must stay clear in every room: the **door lane** `u 4.9..7.1, v 6.4..9.0`, the **board lane** `u 0.3..1.7, v 3.4..5.6` (standing room in front of the stats board) and the **portrait lane** `u 10.3..11.7, v 0.6..8.3` (standing room along the portrait wall). The two side aisles `u 0.3..4.8` and `u 7.2..11.7` at `v 6.4..7.4` connect the spawn to the lanes around the desk.
- Canvas textures repaint only when their cached string changes; scrolling is UV offset only; Kanit 600 and Inter 500 are loaded with `document.fonts.load` and every board repaints once when they resolve, exactly as the exterior plaques do.

## Room template

Floor 12 by 9, walls 3.4 high. Everything in this section is identical in all twelve rooms unless a department section overrides it (floor treatment, wall treatment, ceiling color and light color are the only things a room may override).

1. **Floor**: plane 12.0 x 9.0 at `(6, 0, 4.5)` rx -pi/2, `receiveShadow true`, material per room (default `dept.wall` darkened to 80 percent lightness, roughness 0.9). Rooms that paint a floor use a canvasTex 1024 x 768 (one tile = 85.3 px).
2. **Walls**: four boxes 0.2 thick, 3.4 tall, inner faces at `u 0`, `u 12`, `v 0`, `v 9`: west `0.2 x 3.4 x 9.4` at `(-0.1, 0, 4.5)`, east at `(12.1, 0, 4.5)`, north `12.4 x 3.4 x 0.2` at `(6, 0, -0.1)`, south at `(6, 0, 9.1)`, `dept.wall`, roughness 0.9, `castShadow false`, `receiveShadow true`. The south wall is built as two pieces leaving the door opening `u 5.2..6.8, y' 0..2.2` open (a dark plane fills it, item 7).
3. **Wainscot**: on every wall a panel band 0.9 tall from `y' 0` to `0.9` in `dept.accent` (box 0.04 thick standing 0.02 inside the wall face, roughness 0.8), a chair rail box `0.06 x 0.06` at `y' 0.9` and a baseboard box `0.10 x 0.10` at `y' 0`, both #F8F6F0. A picture rail box `0.04 x 0.04` #F8F6F0 at `y' 3.0` runs around the room.
4. **Ceiling**: plane 12.0 x 9.0 at `(6, 3.4, 4.5)` rx pi/2, color `ceiling` (default #132A42; this is also the value the renderer uses for sky and fog while inside), roughness 1.0. ## Boards

All boards are canvases painted at 4x of their design size, `SRGBColorSpace`, anisotropy 8, `LinearMipmapLinearFilter`, drawn with Kanit 600 for titles and Inter 500 for body (fallback Inter 700, then sans-serif, repainted when the fonts resolve). Rounded rectangles use `roundRect`. Nothing on a board uses an em dash; separators are a middle dot with spaces (` · `).

### Stats board

Canvas 1120 x 640 (world 2.8 x 1.6).

- Background #002D4F filling the canvas inside a 12 px transparent margin, radius 32, with a 2 px inner stroke rgba(226, 233, 238, 0.20).
- Title: department name in Kanit 600 at 92 px white, left aligned at x 64, baseline 128, auto-shrunk so it fits 980 px (Data & Analytics and Embedded Banking land near 84 px). Accent underline 8 px tall, 60 percent of the text width, at y 150, in `dept.accent`.
- Four tiles in a 2 x 2 grid: 464 x 170 each at x 64 and 592, y 184 and 372, fill rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07), radius 20. Label Inter 500 at 34 px #E2E9EE at 85 percent, baseline tile top + 52; value Kanit 600 at 84 px white, baseline tile top + 140. Labels and values:
  - `Agents`: `s.agents` (plain integer).
  - `Runs this week`: `s.runs`.
  - `Clean rate`: `Math.round(100 * (s.runs - s.fails) / s.runs) + '%'` when `s.runs > 0`, otherwise `n/a`.
  - `Working now`: `s.active`; when above 0 a Salmon #E7706F dot r 12 px sits 28 px left of the value.
- Footer: `Signature activity · <activityLabel>` in Inter 500 at 30 px #75CCD0, left at x 64, baseline 612 (activityLabel is the department module's export, for example `Stamping approvals`).
- Cache key: `name|agents|runs|fails|active|fontsReady`. `refreshBoards(rooms, deptStats, agentsByDept)` repaints only boards whose key changed.
